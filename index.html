Frequently Asked Questions

Who may be a candidate for stem cell joint injections?

Adults with ongoing joint or soft tissue discomfort who want a non-surgical option are often evaluated. Candidacy depends on your health history, imaging and goals, so a consultation is the best first step.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients describe mild pressure or temporary soreness. Your provider will explain what to expect and how to care for the area afterward.

How long until I notice a difference?

Healing is gradual and varies from person to person. Some patients notice changes within weeks, while others take longer. Individual results are not guaranteed.

Do you see patients from outside Atlanta?

Yes. Many patients travel from other parts of Georgia and from out of state, and we can help you plan your visit.
